iPhone Mail

  1. Open this page in Safari on your iPhone.
  2. Press and hold on the name "Kate Kors" in the card, then drag the selection handles to cover the photo and all lines. Tap Copy.
  3. Open Settings → Apps → Mail → Signature, delete "Sent from my iPhone" and paste.
  4. If the photo doesn't appear on iPhone, that's an iOS limitation; the text, colours and links still work.

SiteGround Webmail

  1. Open Webmail → Settings → Identities → your address.
  2. Turn on HTML signature, paste the copied card into the Signature box and save.
